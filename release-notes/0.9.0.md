## What's in Public Beta 1

The first public beta, with a new, calmer design.

- **One overview for your disk:** how much is used and free, a bar that shows where the space goes, and a row per tool with what it found
- **A sidebar with every tool:** System Junk and Trash & Downloads under *Clean*, Large Files, Uninstaller and App Updates under *Review*
- **Know what you delete:** every item comes with a short explanation in plain words and a **Safe to Clean** or **Check First** label. Filter by label, sort by size, name or age
- **Clean Safe Items:** one click for everything that's safe to remove
- **Your Mac at a glance:** memory, CPU, battery and network in one quiet line, with details a click away
- **Light and dark:** Fussel follows your Mac's appearance
- **A little life:** Fussel blinks in the sidebar, does a flip when you click him, and cheers when you've cleaned up

## Safety first

Everything Fussel cleans goes to the **Trash first**, so you can always put it back. Only items that are already in the Trash are deleted for good, and Fussel asks before doing that. System files, Apple apps and your main folders are never touched. Nothing in *Large Files* or *Trash & Downloads* is selected until you choose it.

## Install

**Recommended: the installer**

1. Download **Fussel-Installer.pkg** below and open it.
2. Fussel isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks it at first. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to the Fussel installer.
3. Click through the installer. Fussel lands in your Applications folder and appears in the menu bar, next to the clock.
4. Recommended: allow **Full Disk Access** in System Settings › Privacy & Security, so Fussel can reach the Trash and every hidden cache.

**Or: drag and drop**

Open **Fussel.dmg** and drag Fussel onto the Applications folder. Before the first launch, run this once in Terminal:
```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fussel.app
```

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

**Using Fussel:** click the fluff in the menu bar to open the window, right-click it for a quick scan, settings or quit. Closing the window keeps Fussel running in the background.

## Beta notes

- This is a beta: things may still wobble. Please report anything odd as an issue, screenshots help a lot.
- The CPU temperature isn't available on every Mac; Fussel then shows Cool, Warm or Hot instead.
- After an update, macOS may ask for Full Disk Access again.
- Updating: just run the new installer. It quits the old Fussel and replaces it.
- Undo only works while the items are still in the Trash.
- Fussel checks for its own updates on GitHub and only installs an update whose signature it can verify.

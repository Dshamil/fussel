## New in 0.8.1

- **Smoother animations:** Fussel and the fluff around him now move at up to 60 frames per second, and the big banner no longer redraws everything when you move the mouse
- **Fussel watches you better:** his eyes follow the pointer anywhere on the screen, not just over the banner. He leans towards it a little, gets curious (and blushes) when you come close, and looks around on his own when you leave the mouse alone
- **Settings button:** a little gear at the bottom right of the window opens the settings right inside Fussel
- **Even lighter:** all animations stop while the window is hidden or covered by other windows
- **Fixes:**
  - Undo no longer says "Restored" when nothing could be put back (for example after emptying the Trash). You can try again instead
  - The clean-up history can no longer lose its newest entry when two changes happen right after each other
  - Fixed a rare crash that could happen just as the window opened
  - Tests now guard the rules that keep your important folders safe

## What's inside

- **One-click scan** for system junk (caches, logs, Xcode build data), large and old files, the Trash and old downloads
- **Uninstaller** that removes apps together with their support files, caches and preferences
- **Your Mac at a glance:** free disk space, memory, battery, CPU load and temperature, and live network speed with a built-in speed test
- **Free up memory** by quitting the hungriest apps, right from the memory tile
- **Three styles:** White, Dark and Coral, or follow the system automatically
- **Fussel himself:** blinks, follows your pointer, does a flip when you poke him, slurps up files while scanning and celebrates with confetti
- **A playful tutorial** on first launch (right-click the menu bar icon › Show Tutorial to see it again)
- **Undo, history, moods and a weekly reminder** so Fussel feels like a little companion
- **Lives in your menu bar:** no Dock icon. Click the little fluff next to the clock to open Fussel, right-click for a quick scan, the tutorial, settings or quit
- **Starts with your Mac** and quietly waits in the background (turn it off in Settings)

## Safety first

Everything Fussel cleans goes to the **Trash first**, so you can always put it back. Only items that are already in the Trash are deleted for good, and Fussel asks before doing that. System files, Apple apps and your main folders are never touched.

## Install

**Recommended: the installer**

1. Download **Fussel-Installer.pkg** below and open it.
2. This beta isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks it at first. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to the Fussel installer.
3. Click through the installer. Fussel lands in your Applications folder and appears in the menu bar, next to the clock.
4. Recommended: allow **Full Disk Access** in System Settings › Privacy & Security, so Fussel can reach the Trash and every hidden cache.

**Or: drag and drop**

Open **Fussel.dmg** and drag Fussel onto the Applications folder. Before the first launch, run this once in Terminal:
```
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fussel.app
```

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

**Using Fussel:** click the fluff in the menu bar to open or close the window. Right-click it for Scan Mac, Show Tutorial, Settings and Quit. Closing the window keeps Fussel running in the background.

## Beta notes

- This is a beta: things may still wobble. Please report anything odd as an issue, screenshots help a lot.
- The CPU temperature isn't available on every Mac; Fussel then shows Cool, Warm or Hot instead.
- After an update, macOS may ask for Full Disk Access again.
- Updating: just run the new installer. It quits the old Fussel and replaces it.
- Undo only works while the items are still in the Trash.
- Fussel checks for its own updates on GitHub. That works as soon as the GitHub page is public.
- Changing the app icon needs Fussel to be installed with the installer (0.8.0 or newer).
